The process

One sequence.
No shortcuts.

The same four steps produced every log below. If a step is missing, there is no trade — that discipline is the strategy.

🌊
STEP 01

Wait for the sweep

Price raids liquidity above or below a key level, sweeping stops. No sweep, no interest — chasing is how the other side gets paid.

📍
STEP 02

Mark the session POI

The sweep must deliver price into a pre-marked session point of interest, aligned with the higher-timeframe draw. Location filters out most noise.

✅
STEP 03

Confirm the CHoCH

Only a confirmed change of character on the entry timeframe triggers execution — the rule behind the 17–0 high-probability run on EUR/USD.

📓
STEP 04

Journal & grade

Every trade is logged, graded HP / MP / LP and colour-tagged for decision quality — so next month’s plan is written by data, not memory.

actually taught me

Not opinions — patterns that survive being counted, across three instruments that refuse to behave the same way. Each one changed how the next session gets planned.

Confirmation is the edge

Setups graded high-probability before entry — sweep, POI, confirmed CHoCH all present — won 17 of 17 on EUR/USD, averaging +4.48R. The grade predicts the outcome ordering exactly: HP > MP > LP.

Counter-flow fails — usually

DAX trades against MTF order flow won 57.1% and added +5.90R over 7 trades; with the flow, 87.1% and +196.27R. EUR/USD never traded counter-flow at all — 49 of 49 aligned.

On DAX, every non-gray (aggressive / invalid / unclear) trade netted −0.05R — remove them and the win rate jumps 84.1% → 91.7%. On AUD/USD M5/M15 the same filter removes +9.20R of profit. Discipline rules are local, not universal.
